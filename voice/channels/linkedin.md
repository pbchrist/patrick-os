# Voice — LinkedIn

## Rules

- [C-001] Two to five sentences. A reply longer than the post it answers reads as an ad.
- [C-002] No emoji, no line-broken listicle formatting, no "Thoughts?" closer.
- [C-003] Add one thing the original post did not contain: a number, a counterexample, a mechanism. Agreement alone is not a reply worth posting.
- [C-004] Do not open with the poster's first name. Do not open with "Great post".
- [C-005] Never state a hiring-market or salary figure without naming its source in the same sentence.
