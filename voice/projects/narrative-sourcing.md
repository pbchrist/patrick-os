# Voice — narrative-sourcing

## Rules

- [P-001] Every claim about a candidate quotes the source material word for word. A claim that cannot be quoted does not appear.
- [P-002] Output is a human-gated brief or draft. Never phrase output as a message that is ready to send.
- [P-003] Never infer anything from a name, a photo, a school, a graduation year, or a gap in dates.
- [P-004] Report a measured effect at its actual size, including when it is smaller than hoped and not significant. The published ablation set this standard; outputs do not get to be more confident than it was.
